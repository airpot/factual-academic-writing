# Chinese Source Authority

Adoption snapshot: 2026-11-05. Distinguish inspected provisions, contextual analysis and editorial advice. This register is not a comprehensive Chinese grammar code or a legal-compliance certificate.

## Choose the actual basis

1. For a standard-dependent finding, identify the document, edition, applicable provision and actual construction, including qualifications.
2. For a contextual grammar finding, explain the defective relationship using the supplied sentence and context. C1-C7 in chinese-review.md are local checks, not national-standard clauses.
3. For editorial practice or register, identify the basis as advice. Official hosting does not make an author's article an institution-wide rule.
4. If the rule or intended meaning is unavailable, state that limit. Do not invent a source, clause, author, inspection or agreement. A useful optional alternative need not call the original wrong.

The GB/T documents below are recommended national standards. This skill adopts applicable written-form conventions as an editorial default; that adoption does not extend a regulation's legal scope. Preserve protected quotations, code, identifiers and exact strings.

## Inspected written-form rules

### S03. GB/T 15834-2011: 标点符号用法

Published 2011-12-30; effective 2012-06-01. The [official status record](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=22EA6D162E4110E752259661E1A0D0A8) marked it current at adoption. The [registered Ministry of Education reproduction](punctuation-review.md#check-a-registered-link) supplied the inspected scan. Printed pages 2-3, for example, are PDF pages 5-6.

| Guide application | Inspected location | Adopted scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1. Final marks | 4.1.3.1-4.1.3.2; 4.2.3.1-4.2.3.4; A.2 | Judge sentence function and speech act, with qualified question forms. Interrogative wording alone does not make a statement a question. |
| P2. Pauses and lists | 4.4.3.1-4.4.3.3; 4.5.3.1-4.5.3.5 | Use actual syntax/list function. Long-subject pauses can be permitted; quoted-item and adjacent-number rules retain qualifications. |
| P2. Clause separation | 4.6.3.1-4.6.3.3; A.5 | Semicolons have parallel and qualified nonparallel uses. Inspect full-stop-containing list items before choosing terminal marks. |
| P2. Colon scope | 4.7.3.1-4.7.3.5; A.6.1-A.6.3 | Match introductory/summary scope; preserve the qualified nested-level exception. |
| P3. Protected functions | 4.8.3.4-4.8.3.5; A.8; A.9.2; A.13.1-A.13.2 | Distinguish quotation continuity, parenthesis scope, omission and work-title referents. Avoid blanket replacement. |

These paraphrases/applications cover selected inspected clauses, not all layout or punctuation details. Use the authoritative scan if a secondary transcription conflicts with it.

### S04. GB/T 15835-2011: 出版物上数字用法

Published 2011-07-29; effective 2011-11-01. The [official status record](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=F5DAC3377DA99C8D78AE66735B6359C7) marked it current at adoption. The [Ministry of Education reproduction](https://www.moe.gov.cn/ewebeditor/uploadfile/2015/01/13/20150113091154536.pdf) supplied the inspected scan. Its operative body, printed pages 1-6/PDF pages 4-9, was inspected.

| Guide application | Inspected location | Adopted scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| N1. Adjacent-number approximation | 4.2.2; 5.2.1 | Use Chinese numerals without an enumeration comma for this construction. This does not cover every approximate amount or an actual list. |
| N2. Form choice | 4.1-4.3 | Function, established names, rhetorical purpose and context govern the choice, including permitted alternatives. |
| N3. Values and notation | 5.1.2-5.1.6 | Apply the relevant value, range, scale, year/date or time provision. Do not change amounts for appearance. |
| N2-N3. Qualified conventions | 5.1.1; 5.2.5; 5.3 | Grouping and mixed forms have specific contexts. Optional grouping is not a universal requirement. |

The stated scope excludes literary publications and reset ancient texts. Do not impose publication conventions on intentional literary form. Referenced date/time and unit standards were not independently audited here. Exact identifier preservation follows task fidelity, not an invented numeral clause.

## Principles and editorial practice

### S01. Ministry of Education Order 54

The 2023 [信息技术产品国家通用语言文字使用管理规定](https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xxgk/xxgk/zhengce/guizhang/202301/P020230113616145434527.pdf) was promulgated 2023-01-03 and states commencement on 2023-03-01. Articles 2 and 7, including PDF page 3, were inspected. Article 7 addresses language standards and modern Chinese grammatical patterns for products in its scope. It is a relevant sector basis, not an individual syntax verdict or product-compliance certificate. Exhaustive amendment/repeal clearance was not performed.

### S02. General Offices official-document regulations

The [党政机关公文处理工作条例](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2013/content_2344541.htm), 2012 text reproduced in the State Council Gazette, was inspected at Articles 19-20 and scope/commencement provisions. It addresses intended meaning, factual grounding, focused structure, precise expression and review of particulars/written forms. Use its document requirements for that genre. Applying comparable editorial care elsewhere is this skill's adoption, not expanded jurisdiction.

### S05. NDRC-hosted work experience

[公文写作五步法](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/fggz/gbzj/wsgz/201508/t20150812_1105092.html), host date 2015-08-12, appears in the personnel section. Its author is 江宇, Yunnan Jianshui County Party Organization Department. The complete article was inspected. Advice on accurate, concise, appropriate wording and separate content/length/expression revision is professional experience, not an NDRC-wide grammar code.

### S06. People's Daily Overseas Edition commentary

[从录取通知书的“语病”说开去](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrbhwb/pc/content/202508/13/content_30095523.html), 2025-08-13, page 10, author 熊建, was inspected. It distinguishes the cited intelligible but disputed wording from demonstrated hard errors. It supports caution about overcorrection, not a newsroom rulebook or expert consensus on every construction.

### S08. Xinhua-hosted proofreading paper

[以创新型校对机制防范采编数字化的技术性差错](https://www.xinhuanet.com/zgjx/2017-06/15/c_136362026_2.htm), hosted 2017-06-15, credits 重庆记协. Both hosted pages and relevant body sections were inspected. The authors discuss source comparison, contextual semantic judgment and human/software proofreading. It is an author paper, not an internal Xinhua grammar manual. Its recommendation form reports an earlier 中国记者 origin; that original issue was not inspected. The first author's spelling was not verified and is omitted.

## Use and access limits

These bounded offline summaries do not reproduce complete licensed standards or require a network call for every ordinary edit. The adoption audit used official PDFs/the Gazette where some original HTML routes failed; it did not certify every route or every clause.

Use the exact inspected edition when citing a summary. For current status, an unlisted provision or a disputed exception, verify the relevant primary source and identify actual access. If verification cannot settle it, mark the finding unresolved.

For contextual findings, explain the actual predicate, modifier, reference or scope problem. A generic reference to a sector rule or media host does not establish that a sentence is ungrammatical. Keep the confirmed/suggestion/unresolved distinction in [Chinese review](chinese-review.md).
