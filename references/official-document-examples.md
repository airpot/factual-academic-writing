# Official-document Worked Examples

Use with [official-document review](official-document-review.md), the existing modes and the [shared review loop](review-loop.md). All agencies, decisions, packages and values below are fictional teaching material, except the explicitly identified OD-S01 provision. These examples are not execution tests, official approvals or practitioner evidence. Supplied fictional records can govern an exercise without becoming independently verified authorities.

## 1. Editable notice: repair the package and its dependencies

The task permits correction of D1 against the supplied intent record K1 and attachment contents. It is pre-approval editing, not permission to research or choose new policy. K1 fixes the following exercise facts:

> 共复核48条目录数据，甲馆18条、乙馆17条、丙馆13条。正常报送期限为2027年3月11日17时；仅在系统故障且已于该期限前向业务科登记时，可以于3月15日17时前补报。业务科汇总问题，信息科排除故障。附件2的指定名称为《目录数据问题清单》。

Complete editable package D1:

```text
竹浦市档案局关于开展目录数据复核的通知
竹档〔2027〕4号

所属各档案馆：
为核实目录数据，现将复核事项通知如下。
一、复核范围
本次复核目录数据48条，各馆分工见附件1。
二、报送期限
各馆应当于2027年3月12日17时前将问题清单报业务科。系统故障且已于3月11日17时前向业务科登记的，可以于3月15日17时前补报；其他情形不得迟报。报送渠道见第四部分，填报格式见附件2。
四、办理要求
问题清单通过市局收件系统报送。业务科负责汇总，信息科负责排除系统故障；需说明的问题，由报送馆在清单中注明。

附件2
```

Located minimal findings for this authorized edit:

| Location | Example finding and repair |
| --- | --- |
| D1 第二部分首句 | “3月12日”与K1确定的期限不符，改为“3月11日”。此处依获准采用的拟真意图记录改正，不称已核准真实机关期限。 |
| D1 “四、办理要求”及第二部分末句 | 本例已提供完整正文，层次由二跳至四。将“四”改为“三”，并将回指“第四部分”同步改为“第三部分”。仅改标题序号会留下失效引用。 |
| 附件说明第2项 | 已给的附件2题名及K1均为“目录数据问题清单”；将“问题登记表”改为该题名。无需连实际附件题名也改成另一个名称。 |
| 附件1 合计栏 | 18＋17＋13＝48；将49改为48，与正文及K1一致。这是对给定项目的加总校正，不证明项目本身真实。 |
| 附件2 填报说明 | “或”把两个须同时具备的条件改成任一条件。改为“且”，保留登记期限及补报期限；不得改成凡遇故障均可迟报。 |
| D1 “四、办理要求”中的职责句（改号后为第三部分） | 保留。“业务科负责汇总，信息科负责排除系统故障”职责清楚；“需说明的问题，由报送馆在清单中注明”也没有必须补出的未知主体。 |

The affected deadline and exception after correction read:

> 各馆应当于2027年3月11日17时前将问题清单报业务科。系统故障且已于3月11日17时前向业务科登记的，可以于3月15日17时前补报；其他情形不得迟报。

Recheck the body against the attachment note, both dates and times, AND grouping, permission, prohibition and responsible offices. Recheck the heading reference and both attachment names after renumbering. Retain the same-type collective addressee and the effective responsibility sentence. These are local dependency checks; the corrected whole package still goes through the existing review loop.

## 3. Names and quotations: four distinct source claims

The expression-only task protects quoted text. It supplies a fictional roster R3 with “芦汀市档案局” and a manuscript whose title, signature and attachment use that same name. The body defines “市档案局” as its short form but later writes “市档案处” for the same actor. No real agency directory or original signed manuscript is supplied. The manuscript also contains:

> 《党政机关公文处理工作条例》第十五条第（四）项规定：“不得在报告等非请示性公文中夹带申请事项。”

For the quotation comparison, the inspected primary basis is OD-S01's 2012 text, State Council Gazette 2013 issue 7, Article 15(4), identified in [the source table](official-document-review.md#inspected-primary-basis). Its operative word here is “请示”, not “申请”. Do not infer other source checks from this one comparison.

| Item | Accurate example report |
| --- | --- |
| Supplied agency name | “芦汀市档案局”按来稿保留。 |
| Repeated name | 标题、署名、附件中的机构名称内部一致，且与给定拟真名录R3一致；这不等于核实了真实机构名称或规范简称。 |
| Defined short form | 正文已定义“市档案局”，后文同一主体却写为“市档案处”；建议统一为已定义的“市档案局”。此处按给定定义及同一指称作内部修正，不称已核准真实机关的规范简称。 |
| Quotation | 已与实际查阅的2012年《条例》第十五条第（四）项比较：引文“夹带申请事项”与原文“夹带请示事项”不符。仅此处完成了所标版本和条款的来源比较。 |
| Missing original | 未提供签发原件，不能据本次文字核对声称署名、日期或签发行为已经核准。 |

Because this task permits expression edits only and protects the quotation, leave its interior unchanged and place the proposed source correction outside the edited prose:

> 引文疑点另列：如授权据原文更正，应将引文中的“申请”改为“请示”。本次未改受保护引文。

Edit the surrounding expression within its authorized scope. Keep a proposed evidence correction separate even when it concerns an assertion outside the quotation. A smoother quotation does not justify changing protected words or punctuation. Likewise, if the source itself contains an apparent error, do not silently correct that source while presenting its wording as exact.

## 4. Report or request: identify the decision before renaming

Audit this editable excerpt; no decision to change its purpose has been supplied:

```text
竹浦市档案局关于库房监测情况的报告
竹浦市人民政府：
本月已完成两处库房的日常监测，现有设备运行正常。为扩大监测覆盖，拟增设监测点2处，所需经费6万元，请批准。
```

Located finding:

> 正文末句“所需经费6万元，请批准”提出待上级批准的具体事项，与本件标明的“报告”用途冲突。依据OD-S01第八条及第十五条第（四）项，应先确认本件承担汇报还是请示功能。若只作汇报，该批准事项须另作安排；若本件拟请求批准，应围绕该事项形成请示。当前为审计，未删除经费请求，也未直接把标题改为“请示”。

The two routes depend on an author decision; they are not interchangeable minimal expression edits. By contrast, “后续拟继续跟踪温湿度变化” can report a plan. The word “拟” alone does not demonstrate a hidden request or authorize deleting that plan.

## 5. Reply: preserve the scope of an approval and the actors

For this drafting exercise, request Q5 and decision K5 are both supplied fictional records. The reply must express K5 without enlarging it.

> Q5：申请于2027年5月至6月每周五试行夜间查档，开放至20时；仅对预约人数不少于8人的场次开放，预约不足8人的不开放。
>
> K5：同意按来文提出的范围试行。业务科负责业务指导，试行馆负责排班与现场服务；未经另行批准，不得扩大开放日期。

The assistant's own draft reads:

> 同意你馆每晚开放至20时，由业务科负责排班，试行结束后全面推行。

This adds daily opening and subsequent rollout, loses the period and attendance condition, and moves scheduling responsibility. Repair the assistant's draft from both records:

> 同意你馆于2027年5月至6月每周五按来文提出的范围试行夜间查档，仅对预约人数不少于8人的场次开放至20时，预约不足8人的不开放。业务科负责业务指导，试行馆负责排班与现场服务；未经另行批准，不得扩大开放日期。

Check the threshold, period, Friday-only scope, closing time, negative branch and distinct responsibilities in both directions. The first sentence is long but its conditions are recoverable; splitting it is optional if the same scope remains. A reply that says only “同意试行” would omit required conditions. This exercise establishes no real approval.

## 6. Letter: a conventional ending can remain

The supplied context establishes two agencies without a subordinate relationship. The incoming letter asks whether public catalogues can be provided. The answer concerns provision and later coordination; no execution instruction is intended.

Draft:

> 贵单位来函收悉。我局可提供已公开的目录，交接时间另行商定。特此函复，请遵照执行。

Located minimal repair:

> 末句“请遵照执行”引入了本例未设定的执行要求，建议删去该分句及其前置逗号，保留“特此函复。”正文保留“可提供”和“另行商定”的承诺程度与时态，不改为“已提供”或已经确定的交接日期。

The reason is the supplied purpose, not a universal ban on a phrase. “特此函复” is a working conventional expression and needs no replacement with a more conversational ending. If the relationship or purpose were missing, do not decide between 函 and 批复 from the agencies' names alone.

## 7. Minutes, recoverable omissions and disputed expressions

The supplied meeting notes record:

> 甲馆建议将预约门槛降至5人。乙馆认为应继续执行8人门槛。会议未形成统一结论，业务科只负责汇总两馆意见。

An assistant-generated minute says “会议同意将预约门槛降至5人，由业务科组织执行”. Correct that new draft from the notes:

> 会议就预约门槛进行了讨论。甲馆建议降至5人，乙馆认为应继续执行8人门槛；会议未形成统一结论，业务科只负责汇总两馆意见。

Do not turn a proposal into an agreed matter or convert opinion collection into implementation. This is a source-to-draft comparison, not a finding that one of the competing policies is correct.

Separately, retain this effective sentence when its stated completed checks are supplied by the exercise context:

> 经业务科逐项核对，甲馆、乙馆报送的目录编号已与登记簿对应；尚未查清的3项，由原报送馆补充说明。

The topic and participants are recoverable. Do not repeat every actor or split every clause merely to shorten it. The statement's grammatical acceptability does not independently verify that the checks occurred.

In another supplied passage, both archives are identified as participants in a joint check. Its disputed wording is:

> 通过两馆联合核对，使目录编号的对应关系更加清楚。

The event and participants can be recovered, so a clearer alternative alone does not establish a confirmed missing-subject error. An optional suggestion is “两馆联合核对使目录编号的对应关系更加清楚”. Identify it as optional; do not declare every “通过……使……” form wrong or every instance valid. If a different passage leaves the event, actor or intended result unrecoverable, locate that actual gap.

## 8. Missing material: useful partial audit and precise questions

Only a body extract is supplied. It mentions an attachment list on an unseen first page and refers to attachment 2; neither attachment nor a designated final copy is provided. The task asks for a single responsible notifying office, but its designation remains pending in the extract. It also asks for an exact due date, but supplies no receipt date or counting convention.

Extract:

A bounded report can say:

> 首句明示通知责任科室尚待指定，应保留这一待定状态；若本次须落实唯一责任科室，请起草人明确由哪一科承担。第二句的执行者可从“通知各馆”恢复为各馆，不因省略主语就判错；但“未通过复核的”可能指材料或报送单位，需确认例外对象和适用范围，不能据现有节选替起草人决定。
>
> 未提供收件日期及计日约定，不能计算具体截止日。未见第一页、附件2及其口径，也未见实际附件1；不能核对完整标题主送、附件清单是否齐备或正文数值是否与附件一致。未提供指定定稿及变更范围，不能作定稿—待发稿一致结论。可继续审看已给节选的表达，上述项目分别保留为材料或意思待确认项。

Do not invent the missing attachment, replace five days with five working days, select an actor by preference, or label a whole unseen package checked. If the task is short, these located statements suffice without a mandatory form. Review the delivered audit through the same loop; an accurate missing-material finding does not make the underlying document complete.
