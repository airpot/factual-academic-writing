# Chinese Language Review

Use this layer for Chinese prose together with [meaning preservation](meaning-preservation.md) and the shared four-level expression review. Apply modern Chinese grammar to all tasks. Use formal written Chinese for documents, with the register that the task needs. Apply official-document forms only to actual official documents.

## Decide the finding

Identify the task mode, editable text, protected spans, intended meaning and actual context before judging a construction.

| Disposition | Required basis | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmed error | Show an applicable inspected provision or a specific grammatical/semantic conflict that the context establishes. Explain the actual defect. | Correct editable prose in Draft/Edit. In Audit, locate it and propose a correction without changing the source. |
| Suggestion | The original can stand, but another form can improve precision, flow or register. | Preserve a defensible disputed expression; give an optional alternative when useful. Do not count preference as an error. |
| Unresolved | A material referent, relation, meaning or applicable rule is not established. | Identify the precise missing decision. Keep the source meaning open; do not invent a repair. |

No finding is necessary when the text works. A possible trigger is a reason to inspect context, not a verdict. A named institution, common correction list or model confidence does not establish an applicable rule.

Before assigning Confirmed, identify the actual required relation that fails after reading the context. For an apparently missing subject, distinguish a missing written noun from a participant or event that cannot be recovered. In disputed causative openings such as “经…使…” or “通过…使…”, a recoverable actor/event can support a suggestion rather than a hard-error verdict. Do not treat either pattern as always wrong or always valid. A clearer alternative alone does not prove the original ungrammatical.

Keep the distinction between correct audit output and unresolved source content. An accurate question about a missing actor can be a completed audit. It does not make the source unambiguous.

For a material finding, retain its location or exact span, category, reason/basis and minimal repair, optional alternative or question. Use a private record unless the requested delivery needs those details. A short prose-only edit needs no visible table.

Use the [same wording in different contexts](chinese-examples.md#16-recover-an-actor-only-when-the-context-assigns-one) to test a proposed correction: identify what the context supplies, what it contradicts and what it leaves undecided. Apply the same distinction to negation and quantity. For mixed text, decide separately which marks belong to the editable sentence and which belong to protected data or quotation.

## Inspect the construction

The following are local diagnostic checks. They are not clauses of a national grammar standard. Use [source authority](chinese-sources.md) for actual provisions and their limits.

| Check | Locate the defect | Avoid a false correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1. Necessary components and predicate relations | Recover the topic, predicate and participants. Check whether a verb lacks a required object or whether a supplied participant cannot perform the asserted action. | Chinese permits topic-comment sentences, imperatives and recoverable omissions. Do not demand an explicit subject or a subject-verb-object frame in every sentence. Do not classify every “通过…使…” construction by pattern alone. |
| C2. Collocation and parallel parts | Test the subject/predicate, verb/object, modifier/head and each parallel item separately. One verb may fit the first item but fail for another. Check whether compared objects and parallel categories have the same intended basis. | Established technical combinations and conventional metonymy such as “报告指出” can stand. A new predicate must not change the construct, magnitude or evidence strength. |
| C3. Modifier order and scope | Identify what each time, place, quantity and restrictive phrase modifies. Repair competing attachments when the intended relationship is supplied. | More than one Chinese word order can be valid. A dense noun group is not automatically wrong. Do not choose an unprovided source, purpose or actor to unpack it. |
| C4. Sentence frames and clause relations | Check whether incompatible frames leave a predicate or complement without a role. Identify the actual causal, conditional, concessive, parallel, progressive or sequential relation. | A connective cannot create evidence. Paired connectives can be omitted when Chinese grammar and context permit. Do not require “因为…所以…” or “虽然…但是…” to appear in full everywhere. |
| C5. Negation, conditions and quantifiers | Work out the scope of “不、未、并非、都、均、仅、至少、至多” and the grouping of conditions. Compare necessary and sufficient conditions before and after repair. | Multiple negatives can be intentional and correct. Do not turn “不都” into “都不”, OR into AND, or permission into an action that occurred. A grammatical sentence can still contain a factual or logical problem; name that separate problem. |
| C6. Reference, ellipsis and topic chains | Trace each “其、该、这、他” and omitted participant across clauses. Check that a new topic has the needed orientation and that subsequent predicates still attach to the intended participant. | Restore only what the source establishes. If several actors are possible, ask which actor is intended or retain an accurately marked gap. Deliberate literary ambiguity is not an accidental defect. |
| C7. Words and written forms | Check word meaning, lexical function, name, unit and actual syntax. Distinguish a modifier before a noun, an adverbial before a verb and a complement after a predicate when reviewing “的、地、得”. Then check punctuation and numeral function. | Do not replace every “的” by pattern, normalize a name as a quantity, or change an accurate term for variation. A grammatical form can depend on context; inspect that context before declaring a typo. |

Apply paragraph and whole-document checks after these construction checks. Trace the topic, each paragraph's contribution, prerequisite order and the supported conclusion. A sequence of correct sentences can still fail to form a clear document.

## Apply inspected written-form provisions

These applications use the inspected source scope in [S03 and S04](chinese-sources.md#inspected-written-form-rules). They do not certify the complete standards.

- P1. Judge a final mark by the actual speech act and sentence function. Interrogative words inside a statement do not alone require a question mark. Preserve a genuine question, including context-dependent rhetorical use; do not infer sentence function from length or one keyword.
- P2. Judge a comma, enumeration comma, semicolon or colon by its syntactic and discourse function. Use enumeration commas for applicable short parallel elements, not all coordinated material. Semicolons are not restricted to every instance of one fixed clause pattern. A colon's explanatory scope must fit what follows. Keep the standard's qualifications; do not create a rule that every long sentence needs division.
- P3. Check quoted material, brackets, omissions and work titles by what they represent. Source quotations are protected in expression-only work: report an apparent internal error separately. Do not silently move punctuation inside a protected quotation to make its form match a house preference.
- N1. For adjacent Chinese numerals used together to express an approximate amount, do not insert an enumeration comma between them. Distinguish that construction from an actual list, a range or separately named items. Keep the approximate meaning.
- N2. Choose numeral forms by function, established name, genre and permitted local consistency. The numeral standard permits alternatives in specified settings. A preference for Arabic numerals does not make every Chinese numeral an error. Its scope excludes literary publications and reset ancient texts.
- N3. Preserve quantity values, denominators, units, scales, ranges, dates and precision. Treat identifiers, versions, code and exact strings as protected data, not ordinary quantities to normalize. The identifier safeguard comes from task fidelity, not an invented standard clause.

Consult the applicable inspected provision when a finding depends on an exception or a requested clause citation. If the bundled summary does not settle the case, mark that limit. Research further only when the task authorizes it or governing verification rules require it; do not present memory as an inspected source.

Distinguish [statutory scope and software prompts](chinese-sources.md#scoped-authority-additions) from individual editorial decisions. A [recommended word form](chinese-sources.md#s10-gf-1001-2001-recommended-word-forms) can guide editable prose in its scope, and an applicable house style can settle a local choice. Neither is a syntax diagnosis. Verify the relevant entry and leave protected wording intact.

## Repair without changing meaning

Use the existing two-direction comparison rather than a second fact ledger. Check high-risk changes explicitly:

1. Every new assertion or restored participant needs a source basis. A more fluent sentence does not authorize a new cause, actor, intention or approval.
2. Every required original meaning needs a place after the repair. Retain attribution, modality, conditions, negation, scope, quantities and actual execution state.
3. Check each parallel item and each linked clause again. Splitting a sentence can detach an exception or leave a possibility marker governing the wrong claim.
4. Compare exact protected spans separately from editable explanation. A grammar judgment does not authorize changing a quote, schema or identifier.
5. Read the whole repaired passage in its actual genre. Remove an unnecessary repair if it weakens the author's position, introduces repetition or destroys an intentional effect.

For Draft, correct errors in the assistant's own answer. For Edit, keep source assertions and give substantive evidence corrections separately unless authorized. For Audit, preserve the requested source carrier and put findings outside it. A polished audit does not validate the underlying facts.

## Review and deliver

Use the same complete [review loop](review-loop.md); do not add a Chinese-only nested loop. Each full round checks the entire artifact's meaning, support, grammar decisions, four clarity levels, voice, task constraints and protected content.

Review both misses and false corrections. A confirmed editable error blocks a clean artifact; a justified rejection of a suspected error does not. An accurately reported source decision gap is different from an unresolved defect in the delivered audit.

Review each diagnosis separately from its proposed wording: can the stated conflict be demonstrated, or does the explanation merely repeat a trigger pattern? Reclassify an unsupported hard-error verdict even when its proposed sentence is fluent and faithful. Keep optional clarity edits consistent with the requested editing intent.

At the existing cap, return useful provisional work with the actual remaining defect. Do not certify perfect grammar, source truth or human comprehension from a scanner result or review count. See [the Chinese examples](chinese-examples.md) for repairs, valid expressions and unresolved cases.
