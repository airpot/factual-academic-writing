# Same-material Writing Examples

These examples use synthetic teaching material. They assert no actual study or system.
Use the decisions that fit the task. Do not copy the examples' facts, positions, or structure into another manuscript.
The example prose is teaching text, not a mandatory style for every discipline.

## 1. Notes to a developed paragraph

Supplied notes:

- Interviews with 18 university teachers in 2025.
- Twelve reported checking tool suggestions against original sources.
- The interviews did not measure error rates or learning outcomes.
- Author position: checking a suggestion and deciding to adopt it are separate steps.
- Research question: how do these teachers decide when suggestions and original sources disagree?

Weak draft, quoted as an example of ineffective expression:

> 随着智能技术的快速发展，学术实践迎来了新的机遇与挑战。18名教师参加访谈，12人进行了核对。这充分表明该问题具有重要意义。本文关注教师如何判断不一致，围绕这一问题展开研究，探讨教师面对不一致时的判断。

Revised introduction:

> 核对工具建议与决定采用建议，是两个不同的判断环节。2025年对18名高校教师的访谈中，12人报告会对照原始来源核对工具建议。本文关注这些教师在建议与原始来源不一致时如何作出取舍；访谈材料用于考察其自报判断过程。

The first sentence states the supplied author position. The evidence establishes the bounded setting.
The last sentence names the objective once and identifies the material as self-report.
The revision removes empty significance and repeated objectives. It does not turn reported checking into an observed action or measured benefit.
The absence of an error-rate measure need not dominate this introduction. Keep it where a claim about error rates needs that boundary.

Discussion from the same notes:

> 访谈记录了教师自报的核对经历，但不能代替对采用决定的说明。区分这两个环节，有助于明确本文所问的取舍问题：核对是否发生与核对之后如何判断，需要分别说明其依据。现有笔记未提供具体取舍策略，尚不能据此完成对判断过程的解释。

The discussion develops the supplied distinction instead of repeating the participant counts.
It identifies the missing answer without inventing a strategy or claiming an effect.
This is a bounded partial discussion, not a complete finding that the supplied notes do not contain.

## 2. Three sources to a synthesis

Supplied source summaries:

| Source and locator | Material |
| --- | --- |
| S1, supplied summary | Ten rural teachers reported that manual checking took time. Interviews, no efficiency or trust measure. |
| S2, supplied summary | Sixty urban teachers reported high trust in a questionnaire. No observation of checking. |
| S3, supplied summary | Observation and interviews with five teachers. All reported high trust, but checking varied. No causal experiment. |

Author question: can reported trust represent actual checking?
Author position: distinguish the two.

Summary stack, quoted as an ineffective organization:

> S1研究了人工核对，S2研究了信任，S3研究了核对差异。这些研究提供了丰富视角，具有重要启示。

Synthesis:

> 这些材料涉及不同层面的证据，不能直接用自报信任代表核对行为。S2的问卷记录了城市教师的信任态度，未观察实际核对；S3中的五名教师均自报高信任，核对情况却不同。S3涉及观察与访谈，但摘要未交代这一差异具体来自哪种记录。S1关于核对耗时的访谈涉及时间负担，不能替代效率或信任测量。因此，讨论信任与核对的关系时，需要分别说明态度报告与核对记录的依据。

The paragraph groups the sources by what their evidence can establish.
It explains how S2 and S3 relate to the question, then places S1 in its distinct role.
It adds no combined sample estimate, universal consensus, or causal account.
It does not infer which method established S3's checking differences or equate a high-trust category with equal attitudes.
Source IDs stand for supplied materials. Use the manuscript's real citation form when available.

## 3. Deep rewriting with author voice

Supplied material: four of 80 records did not match the source records. The comparison only inspected matching.
No intervention or performance comparison was supplied.
Author position: inspect unmatched records before use. Do not infer overall performance.
Author voice sample: "We report what the records show. We keep the interpretation narrow."

Before, quoted as ineffective expression:

> In the rapidly evolving landscape of digital scholarship, this holistic analysis offers profound insights. Four of 80 records did not match. Our focus is on unmatched records, and we center this inquiry on cases that did not match, providing an important foundation for future exploration.

After:

> Four of 80 records did not match the source records. These records need inspection before use. The comparison concerns matching and does not establish overall performance.

The revision uses direct statements and keeps the supplied position.
It removes ornamental framing and three expressions of the same focus.
It preserves the four-of-80 result without adding failure causes, tool design, or user outcomes.
The sample guides directness. Its wording and research assertions are not a template to copy.

## 4. Interpretation with a counterreading

Supplied fictional excerpt:

> “灯还亮着，屋里却没有回应。”

Author interpretation: the image separates signs of presence from an actual response.
Alternative: the people may simply be absent.
No surrounding text or author statement is supplied.

Analysis:

> “灯还亮着，屋里却没有回应。”将持续的光亮与回应的缺失并置。“却”使两者形成反差，可以读作存在迹象与实际回应之间的分离。另一种读法是人已离开，亮着的灯与无人回应只是同时存在的场景状态。

The analysis explains the textual relation and preserves the quotation exactly.
It keeps the author's reading conditional and examines the supplied alternative.
It does not require an empirical experiment to permit textual interpretation.
The prose keeps both readings possible without introducing an unclaimed motive or a source-checking disclaimer.

## 5. Keep a paragraph that already works

Supplied paragraph:

> 我们先核对表格编号，再检查单位，最后登记疑义。此外，直接引文保持原样，如“这里还要核对。不能省略。”。

For a light expression edit, the returned paragraph can remain exactly the same.
The order describes a real sequence. The additive transition has a distinct purpose.
Uniform sentence lengths and quotation-internal periods need no repair.
If the user requests prose only, return this paragraph without an unrelated report of unperformed source checks.

## 6. Affirmative discussion from adequate results

Supplied material: twelve editors each checked one citation with a version difference.
All checked the version date and then the original page. Seven adopted the original wording. Five deferred and recorded a question.
No follow-up or causal experiment was supplied.
Author question: what checking steps and adoption decisions occurred in these observed cases?
Author position: completed checking and adoption decisions should be reported separately.

Discussion:

> 本次观察中，12名编辑核对引文时，均先查看版本日期，再对照原始页面；随后，7人采用原始表述，5人暂缓并登记疑义。相同的核对步骤对应不同的采用决定，说明完成核对与决定采用应分开报告。

The observation scope is explicit. The evidence supports the supplied author distinction.
The paragraph does not claim an effect or a cause, so it needs no added catalogue of absent effect experiments.
It does not explain the reasons for the decision differences. Do not invent them to extend the discussion.

## 7. Combine repeated limits, keep distinct limits

Supplied draft, quoted to demonstrate duplicated qualifications:

> 11人均完成核对，4人暂缓采用。核对完成与采用不同。未做效果比较，不能说质量提高。没有效果比较，无法认定质量改善。未判定内容正确性，暂缓不能证明建议错误。没有判断正确性，不能把暂缓视作错误证据。

Revision:

> 11人均完成核对，4人暂缓采用，说明核对完成与采用并不相同。未做效果比较，不能据此认定质量提高；未判定内容正确性，也不能将暂缓采用视为建议错误的证据。

Each of the two boundaries appears once. Neither is removed.
An effect comparison and a correctness judgment constrain different conclusions.
The edit keeps supplied counts, negation, and author position instead of banning caution words.
