# Same-material Writing Examples

These examples use synthetic teaching material. They assert no actual study or system.
Use the decisions that fit the task. Do not copy the examples' facts, positions, or structure into another manuscript.
The example prose is teaching text, not a mandatory style for every discipline.

## 1. Notes to a developed paragraph

Supplied notes:

- Interviews with 18 university teachers in 2025.
- Twelve reported checking tool suggestions against original sources.
- The interviews did not measure error rates or learning outcomes.
- Author position: checking a suggestion and deciding to adopt it are separate steps.
- Research question: how do these teachers decide when suggestions and original sources disagree?

Weak draft, quoted as an example of ineffective expression:

> 随着智能技术的快速发展，学术实践迎来了新的机遇与挑战。18名教师参加访谈，12人进行了核对。这充分表明该问题具有重要意义。本文关注教师如何判断不一致，围绕这一问题展开研究，探讨教师面对不一致时的判断。

Revised introduction:

> 核对工具建议与决定采用建议，是两个不同的判断环节。2025年对18名高校教师的访谈中，12人报告会对照原始来源核对工具建议。本文关注这些教师在建议与原始来源不一致时如何作出取舍；访谈材料用于考察其自报判断过程。

The first sentence states the supplied author position. The evidence establishes the bounded setting.
The last sentence names the objective once and identifies the material as self-report.
The revision removes empty significance and repeated objectives. It does not turn reported checking into an observed action or measured benefit.
The absence of an error-rate measure need not dominate this introduction. Keep it where a claim about error rates needs that boundary.

Discussion from the same notes:

> 访谈记录了教师自报的核对经历，但不能代替对采用决定的说明。区分这两个环节，有助于明确本文所问的取舍问题：核对是否发生与核对之后如何判断，需要分别说明其依据。

The discussion develops the supplied distinction instead of repeating the participant counts.
The supplied notes contain no concrete choice strategies. Keep this access limit outside the manuscript; it does not establish that the interviews omitted them.
This is a bounded partial discussion, not a complete finding that the supplied notes do not contain.

## 2. Three sources to a synthesis

Supplied source summaries:

| Source and locator | Material |
| --- | --- |
| S1, supplied summary | Ten rural teachers reported that manual checking took time. Interviews, no efficiency or trust measure. |
| S2, supplied summary | Sixty urban teachers reported high trust in a questionnaire. No observation of checking. |
| S3, supplied summary | Observation and interviews with five teachers. All reported high trust, but checking varied. No causal experiment. |

Author question: can reported trust represent actual checking?
Author position: distinguish the two.

Summary stack, quoted as an ineffective organization:

> S1研究了人工核对，S2研究了信任，S3研究了核对差异。这些研究提供了丰富视角，具有重要启示。

Synthesis:

> 信任态度与核对行为涉及不同层面的证据，不能直接用自报信任代表核对行为。S2的问卷记录了城市教师的信任态度，未观察实际核对；S3报告五名教师均自报高信任，核对情况却不同，但这一差异的证据类型尚不明确，不能直接视为已观察的行为差异。S1关于核对耗时的访谈涉及时间负担，不能替代效率或信任测量。因此，讨论信任与核对的关系时，需要分别说明态度报告与核对记录的依据。

The paragraph groups the sources by what their evidence can establish.
It explains how S2 and S3 relate to the question, then places S1 in its distinct role.
It adds no combined sample estimate, universal consensus, or causal account.
S3 uses observation and interviews. Its summary does not identify which records establish checking differences.
Attribute the difference to S3's report. Keep this evidence-type boundary beside the behavioral inference it limits.
Keep general access records outside manuscript prose. Do not hide a missing premise needed for the argument.
Do not equate a high-trust category with equal attitudes.
Source IDs stand for supplied materials. Use the manuscript's real citation form when available.

## 3. Deep rewriting with author voice

Supplied material: four of 80 records did not match the source records. The comparison only inspected matching.
No intervention or performance comparison was supplied.
Author position: inspect unmatched records before use. Do not infer overall performance.
Author voice sample: "We report what the records show. We keep the interpretation narrow."

Before, quoted as ineffective expression:

> In the rapidly evolving landscape of digital scholarship, this holistic analysis offers profound insights. Four of 80 records did not match. Our focus is on unmatched records, and we center this inquiry on cases that did not match, providing an important foundation for future exploration.

After:

> Four of 80 records did not match the source records. These records need inspection before use. The comparison concerns matching and does not establish overall performance.

The revision uses direct statements and keeps the supplied position.
It removes ornamental framing and duplicated statements of focus while retaining the result.
It preserves the four-of-80 result without adding failure causes, tool design, or user outcomes.
The sample guides directness. Its wording and research assertions are not a template to copy.

## 4. Interpretation with a counterreading

Supplied fictional excerpt:

> “灯还亮着，屋里却没有回应。”

Author interpretation: the image separates signs of presence from an actual response.
Alternative: the people may simply be absent.
No surrounding text or author statement is supplied.

Analysis:

> “灯还亮着，屋里却没有回应。”将持续的光亮与回应的缺失并置。“却”使两者形成反差，可以读作存在迹象与实际回应之间的分离。另一种读法是人可能不在屋里，亮着的灯与无人回应只是同时存在的场景状态。

The analysis explains the textual relation and preserves the quotation exactly.
It keeps the author's reading conditional and examines the supplied alternative.
It does not require an empirical experiment to permit textual interpretation.
The prose keeps both readings possible without introducing an unclaimed motive or a source-checking disclaimer.

## 5. Keep a paragraph that already works

Supplied paragraph:

> 我们先核对表格编号，再检查单位，最后登记疑义。此外，直接引文保持原样，如“这里还要核对。不能省略。”。

For a light expression edit, the returned paragraph can remain exactly the same.
The order describes a real sequence. The additive transition has a distinct purpose.
Uniform sentence lengths and quotation-internal periods need no repair.
If the user requests prose only, return this paragraph without an unrelated report of unperformed source checks.

## 6. Affirmative discussion from adequate results

Supplied material: twelve editors each checked one citation with a version difference.
All checked the version date and then the original page. Seven adopted the original wording. Five deferred and recorded a question.
No follow-up or causal experiment was supplied.
Author question: what checking steps and adoption decisions occurred in these observed cases?
Author position: completed checking and adoption decisions should be reported separately.

Discussion:

> 本次观察中，12名编辑核对引文时，均先查看版本日期，再对照原始页面；7人采用原始表述，5人暂缓并登记疑义。相同的核对步骤对应不同的采用决定，说明完成核对与决定采用应分开报告。

The observation scope is explicit. The evidence supports the supplied author distinction.
The paragraph does not claim an effect or a cause, so it needs no added catalogue of absent effect experiments.
It does not explain the reasons for the decision differences. Do not invent them to extend the discussion.

## 7. Combine repeated limits, keep distinct limits

Supplied draft, quoted to demonstrate duplicated qualifications:

> 11人均完成核对，4人暂缓采用。核对完成与采用不同。未做效果比较，不能说质量提高。没有效果比较，无法认定质量改善。未判定内容正确性，暂缓不能证明建议错误。没有判断正确性，不能把暂缓视作错误证据。

Revision:

> 11人均完成核对，4人暂缓采用，说明核对完成与采用并不相同。未做效果比较，不能据此认定质量提高；未判定内容正确性，也不能将暂缓采用视为建议错误的证据。

Each of the two boundaries appears once. Neither is removed.
An effect comparison and a correctness judgment constrain different conclusions.
The edit keeps supplied counts, negation, and author position instead of banning caution words.

## 8. A Chinese introduction with a bounded research position

Synthetic materials:

- A: interviews with 12 service staff. They reported checking whether submitted forms were complete. No adoption decisions were observed.
- B: a questionnaire of 25 staff about clarity of online instructions. No checking behavior was observed.
- Present study: observation of 16 staff reviewing one disputed record each. All checked the record version and then its original page.
- Nine adopted the original wording. Seven deferred and recorded a question. Reasons for the different decisions were not recorded.
- Author question: which checking steps and adoption decisions occurred in these cases?
- Author position: checking and adoption should be reported separately.

Complete introduction:

> 核对记录与决定采用记录，是服务人员处理争议材料时的两个环节。前者涉及对版本和原始页面的检查，后者涉及是否采用原始表述。将两者分开报告，才能看清工作人员完成了哪些检查，又作出了什么决定。
>
> 既有研究分别涉及工作人员自报的完整性核对和对线上指引清晰程度的评价。A中的12名工作人员报告会检查表格是否完整，但访谈未观察采用决定；B中的25名工作人员评价线上指引，问卷未观察核对行为。这些研究说明了各自记录的对象，不能合并为工作人员从核对到采用的完整过程。
>
> 本研究观察16名工作人员各处理一项有争议的记录，考察这些个案中发生的核对步骤及采用决定。研究问题限定于已观察的处理过程，不以清晰程度评价代替实际行为，也不将完成核对等同于决定采用。

Paragraph functions: define the supplied distinction, compare prior evidence, then position the actual observation question.
The last paragraph states the question without inventing a new causal objective or universal literature gap.
Do not insert these synthetic source labels into real research. Use actual supplied references.

## 9. A Chinese discussion with necessary repetition and separate limits

Use the present-study materials in example 8. No follow-up, quality comparison or reasons for decisions are supplied.

Complete discussion:

> 16名工作人员处理争议记录时，都先查看版本，再对照原始页面；9人采用原始表述，7人暂缓并登记疑义。相同的核对步骤对应不同的采用决定，支持将核对过程与处置结果分开报告。核对步骤说明工作人员检查了什么，采用记录说明作出的决定，两类信息共同呈现了本次观察的处理过程。
>
> 这种区分也限定了对结果的解释。现有记录没有说明9人与7人作出不同决定的原因，不能将差异解释为信任程度或能力差异。暂缓并登记疑义是已观察的处置，不是内容错误的证据；同样，采用原始表述也不等于其内容正确。
>
> 对服务过程的记录而言，本次观察的价值在于同时保留检查步骤和采用决定。工作人员已经完成核对这一事实，可以与尚未采用材料的状态并存。记录这两类信息，有助于避免用“已核对”概括整个处置结果；现有材料未提供处理质量的比较结果，尚不能据此认定这一记录方式已经提高质量。

Reverse outline:

| Paragraph | Job | New contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Report the observations and their relation | Same steps can accompany different decisions. |
| 2 | Interpret decision states within the evidence | Neither adopting nor deferring establishes content correctness. |
| 3 | Explain the supplied recording contribution | Completed checking and pending adoption can coexist; no quality-comparison result is supplied, so these records do not establish quality improvement. |

The third paragraph revisits “checking and adoption” for the recording consequence, not merely another wording of the opening distinction.
Its quality boundary differs from the second paragraph's correctness boundary. Neither should be deleted as a repeated caution word.
In a shorter requested discussion, combine useful points without replacing the author's positive recording judgment with only limitations.

## 10. Same facts, different valid voices

Synthetic facts: a page lists required documents before a visit. Eight of 13 assisted users completed booking.
The 13 assisted users are part of a 48-user cohort. Remaining booking outcomes are not recorded.
Author position: retain assistance alongside the page. No measured effect on travel is supplied.

Sample A, voice only: “We report the observed steps. We keep their results distinct.”
Profile: first-person author judgment, direct separation of observations. No incidental sentence-length rule.

> 页面在到访前列出所需材料，协助渠道则回应需要帮助的使用者。在48名使用者中，13人接受协助，其中8人完成预约。我们据此主张保留协助渠道；其余预约结果未记录。

Sample B, voice only: “这一区分使流程中的两个环节各得其位，并为理解后续记录提供了必要前提。”
Profile: formal relation-oriented exposition. The sample provides no new study results.

> 到访前展示材料要求与提供协助，分别构成信息告知和辅助办理的安排。48名使用者中的13人接受协助，其中8人完成预约。这些记录支持作者保留协助渠道的主张；其余预约结果未记录。

Both versions preserve the same supplied counts, overlap, outcome limit and positive author position.
They make no claim about reduced travel, so an appended catalogue of absent travel studies would add no needed boundary.
They need not use identical rhythm or author self-reference to be valid academic prose.

## 11. Similar phrases with different functions

Synthetic material: staff first checked dates, then units. They also preserved direct quotations. No effectiveness measure was supplied.

| Passage | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| 工作人员先检查日期，再检查单位。此外，他们保留直接引文的原有表述。 | Keep. The transition adds a separate action. |
| 工作人员检查日期与单位，这具有重要意义，产生了重要启示，为后续工作提供了重要基础。 | Revise. The supplied material establishes actions, not these evaluations. |
| 这些记录说明了检查步骤，不能据此判断检查是否提高了处理质量。 | Keep when a quality inference is at issue. The qualification limits that inference. |
| 工作人员已检查日期与单位。这不能证明学习提高、出行减少或人物动机改变。 | Do not add these unrelated cautions in a draft about checking steps. In expression-only edits, preserve distinct original conclusions unless substantive omission is authorized. |

An unchanged good paragraph is a valid result. Neither academic phrase frequency nor a scanner hit decides the editorial outcome.

## 12. Boundaries without an editorial ending

Synthetic sources: study A assessed twelve trainees with four written comprehension questions; the mean was three correct answers. It did not test practical operation. Study B observed eight other trainees in a simulation; five completed both checking steps. It did not test written comprehension. The author proposes a combined assessment in one group, limited to these training settings; no field-wide gap is claimed.

Expression-edit target:

> 研究A中，12名学员完成四道理解题，平均答对三题。但它没有操作测试，不能说明操作能力。研究B中，另8名学员参加模拟，5人完成两项核查。但它没有理解测验，不能说明书面理解。两项研究没有共同参与者，不能形成同一个体从理解到操作的证据。本文建议在同一组学员中结合理解测验与模拟核查。所给材料仅涉及这两种培训情境，不能说整个领域缺少研究。

Revised passage:

> 两项研究分别记录了书面理解和模拟核查。研究A的12名学员完成四道理解题，平均答对三题；测验没有覆盖实际操作。研究B则观察另8名学员参加模拟，其中5人完成两项核查，但未设书面理解测验。两种测量有助于区分理解表现与核查行为；由于参与者不同，现有结果无法呈现同一个体从理解到操作的过程。
>
> 本文据此建议在同一组学员中结合理解测验与模拟核查，并将检验范围限定于上述两种培训情境。这个具体问题不构成对整个领域研究空白的判断。

The revision keeps both unmeasured constructs, the separate groups, the proposal and the field-wide-gap boundary. It changes their organization, not their strength. The last sentence remains because this expression edit must preserve the original distinct conclusion; in a draft, a clearly bounded question may need no such additional sentence. Do not require a two-paragraph layout for other tasks.

## 13. Unpack relations without changing the construct

Synthetic material: an author defines archive retrieval reliability through result relevance, version consistency and traceable provenance.
The author plans to compare two assurance workflows. No comparison result is supplied.

Title to edit:

> 档案检索可靠性保障流程比较分析

Revision:

> 比较两套保障档案检索可靠性的流程

An accompanying sentence can state the definition:

> 本文以结果相关性、版本一致性和出处可追溯性界定检索可靠性，拟比较两套保障流程，尚无比较结果。

“怎样让档案更容易找” is not an equivalent title. Ease of finding an archive does not retain the defined reliability construct.
The revision unpacks the comparison and its object. It does not claim that either workflow improved reliability.
Where the material does not establish the relations within a noun chain, do not select a convenient interpretation only for fluency.

## 14. Keep conditions with the claim they restrict

Synthetic material: a coefficient applies only to instruments with zero calibration. Its scope excludes high-temperature measurements.
The data sheet recommends separate validation for high-temperature conditions.

Faithful Chinese prose:

> 该系数仅适用于经过零点校准的仪器，不适用于高温测量。数据说明书建议另行验证高温条件。

“仪器经过零点校准。该系数可用于测量。” loses the restricted scope and the high-temperature exclusion.
Short sentences do not repair that loss. Keep the recommendation too when it belongs to the requested passage.
“该系数适用于” and “说明书建议” are normal abstract-subject uses in this technical context.
Do not add an unknown researcher or intention to make either subject human.

## 15. Unpack English dependencies without claiming completion

Synthetic material: a laboratory plans a test of covariance-estimate stability across repeated model fits.
The model uses measurements from a second sensor. The fits have not been run.
The planned test addresses repeatability, not external validity.

Edit target:

> The laboratory plans a second-sensor-measurement model repeated-fit covariance-estimate stability test, addressing repeatability rather than external validity; the fits have not been run.

Revision:

> The laboratory plans to test whether covariance estimates remain stable across repeated fits. The model uses measurements from the second sensor. The fits have not been run. The test concerns repeatability, not external validity.

The revision names the test and gives the model's measurement source separately. It keeps the technical constructs and the planned action.
“The laboratory confirmed stable estimates” would invent completion and a result.
“The test shows the model works” would replace repeatability with an unspecified broader judgment.
Added relation words are useful here. The target's length or semicolon alone does not establish the defect.

## 16. Keep effective professional prose and identify the actual check

Synthetic material and acceptable English prose:

> The report recommends a sensitivity analysis. The analysis addresses parameter uncertainty, not measurement error.

The recommendation, object and boundary are clear. Preserve the terms for the intended technical reader.
“The authors want to understand what went wrong” would add a person, intention and failure that the material does not supply.
No rewrite is needed only because “report” is inanimate or the terms are unusual in conversation.

A text-only review may record that the recommendation and its boundary remain easy to identify.
It must not claim “I listened to the passage” without audio use.
An actual audio check can find awkward phrasing, but smooth delivery does not verify the claim or the reader's understanding.
Keep such checking notes outside a prose-only response.
