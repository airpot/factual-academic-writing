# Official-document Language Review

Use this reference only for official-document drafting, language review or final-copy collation. Keep the existing Draft/Edit/Audit modes, [C1-C7 Chinese checks](chinese-review.md), [meaning safeguards](meaning-preservation.md) and [one shared review loop](review-loop.md). Other prose does not acquire official-document conventions through this route.

## Establish the task and stage

Identify the intended action, genre, issuer, audience/addressee and supplied relationship between the agencies. Establish which text is editable, which spans are protected, and which version or source governs each comparison. Infer routine choices from clear context. Ask only for a missing decision that would change the result.

| Stage | Operation |
| --- | --- |
| Draft or editable pre-approval text | Draft from the supplied decisions; in Edit improve expression within authorization; in Audit locate defects and propose repairs without altering the source. Do not decide an unprovided policy intention. |
| Designated final baseline and issue copy | Identify the designated baseline, issue copy and authorized changes, including their versions and locations. Compare wording and attachments. Restore a transcription difference only within the correction authority; otherwise propose the exact restoration. Do not polish finalized wording merely because another form is smoother. |
| Stage or baseline not established | Complete supported language checks, but mark the affected version/collation decision open. A file named “final” or the newest timestamp alone does not establish approval. |

Distinguish the task's designated baseline from independently authenticated approval. A supplied synthetic approval record is exercise data. It does not prove real agency approval.

## Check purpose, genre and expression

Check whether the title, addressee, body and requested action serve the same purpose. Use the actual agency relationship and context, rather than an ending phrase alone, to judge the genre. OD-S01 Article 8 supplies these purposes within its scope:

| Genre | Purpose and language question |
| --- | --- |
| 通知 | Convey matters for execution or notice, including forwarding documents. Can the relevant recipients recover what to do and when? |
| 请示 | Request instructions or approval from a superior. Is the requested decision identifiable? Article 15(4) requires one matter per request. |
| 报告 | Report work or circumstances, or answer a superior's inquiry. Article 15(4) prohibits embedding requests for instructions/approval in a non-request document. Flag an actual requested decision; do not treat every mention of a need as a request. |
| 批复 | Answer a subordinate's request. Does the reply address that request without expanding its granted scope or changing its conditions? |
| 函 | Consult, inquire, answer, or request/answer approval between agencies without a subordinate relationship. Do not invent that relationship to select the genre. |
| 纪要 | Record the main meeting circumstances and agreed matters. Distinguish a proposal, discussion, agreement and assigned action; attendance or silence does not itself establish approval. |

If genre and body conflict, locate the conflict and the required author decision. Do not silently remove a request, invent approval, or rename the document as if its purpose were settled. Check title components and addressee wording against OD-S01 Article 9(7)-(8) when applicable. A supplied collective designation can be valid; do not demand a list of full agency names in every case.

Apply C1-C7 to the whole passage, then check its paragraph sequence and execution requirements. In particular:

- Trace who requests, decides, executes, coordinates, receives and reports. Recoverable omissions can remain; supply an actor only when the material establishes it. A coordinating office does not automatically become the responsible decision-maker.
- Preserve the force and scope of “应当、必须、可以、不得、原则上、除外”. Do not interchange obligation, permission and prohibition, remove a qualification, or turn an exception into a general rule. These checks are contextual analysis, not a new national modal-word grammar code.
- Check the grouping of conditions, negation and quantifiers, including “且/或” and the scope of an exception. A clearer split must keep the same conditions attached to the same actor and action.
- Check dates and deadlines across the document and attachments. Preserve calendar/workday units, time of day, triggering event and any stated inclusion rule. If “收到后五日内” lacks a supplied counting convention that matters to the answer, ask for it rather than invent a due date or holiday rule.
- Check conventional expressions in their actual function. Accurate formal wording, an effective long sentence and a clear topic chain need no repair. No single ending, sentence length or ornamental official style is mandatory.
- Keep full names, normalized abbreviations, titles and technical terms stable. Check first-use orientation when needed and whether two abbreviations identify one body or different bodies. An understandable short form is not thereby an independently verified official abbreviation.

## Separate preservation from source comparison

For names, places, dates, figures, document titles/numbers and quotations, state what was actually checked. Apply the distinction to each material field; one checked quotation does not verify the entire document.

| Status | Warranted statement |
| --- | --- |
| Preserved as supplied | The wording/value was retained from the supplied text. No source-accuracy claim follows. |
| Internally consistent | The inspected occurrences agree within the supplied body, tables or attachments, on the same basis. Agreement alone does not establish external accuracy. |
| Compared with an identified inspected source | Identify the actual document/record, edition or version, locator and compared span/value; say whether they match. A comparison with a supplied fictional roster remains comparison with that roster, not independent official verification. |
| Insufficient material | Identify the missing source, page, version, attachment, scope definition or decision, and the claim it prevents. Do not describe an unseen original as checked. |

Statuses can coexist: a name can be preserved and internally consistent while its official form remains unverified. Compare quantities only after checking unit, period, population, overlap, denominator and rounding. Different figures can be correct on different bases. Matching totals do not prove the underlying records true.

For a protected quotation, compare wording, internal punctuation, attribution and locator separately from its editable introduction. In expression-only work, retain a supplied quotation even if the inspected source reveals a mismatch; report the discrepancy and proposed source correction separately. Do not silently repair a source's apparent typo inside a protected quotation. A substantive correction needs task authority.

Use the bounded sources below for provisions they actually support. For an uninspected provision, disputed exception or current-status claim, obtain the required primary material under the governing task rules or mark the limit. The source register is not exhaustive legal clearance.

## Collate the complete available package

Establish the package inventory before claiming whole-package coverage: title, addressee, body, heading levels, cross-references, attachment list, actual attachment versions and issue copy. A listed attachment is not evidence that its content was received. Locate each missing or unreadable item and continue the checks that the available material supports.

1. Match title, addressee and body purpose, including repeated agency names and the scope of the requested action.
2. Trace heading levels and references by their actual targets. Check omissions, duplication, stale references and unintended level changes; do not renumber an intentional extract as if it were a complete document.
3. Match each attachment number and name to an actual attachment and its title/version. Check missing, extra, duplicate or misnamed items. A related attachment is not necessarily the designated one.
4. Compare shared actors, places, dates, totals, units, conditions and obligations across the body, attachment descriptions and actual contents. Locate both sides of a conflict. Identify the controlling material or the precise missing decision instead of choosing a convenient value.
5. For final-copy work, compare the whole available issue package with the designated baseline plus identified authorized changes. Preserve raw differences before any permitted normalization. Apply an authorized change only to its stated scope; a changed title does not authorize a changed deadline.

Classify final-copy observations separately:

| Observation | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Actual wording/value difference | Quote or locate both versions; identify an authorized change or propose restoration/decision. Do not dismiss a changed negation or punctuation mark as layout. |
| Display or layout-only difference | Describe only what is visible in the inspected render or declared representation. A shown line wrap can leave wording unchanged; text extraction alone cannot certify page layout, typography, seals or printing quality. |
| Error already present in the baseline | Locate it in both versions and report it separately. It is not a newly introduced transcription error and final-copy checking does not authorize silently rewriting the baseline. |
| Missing baseline, change authority or material | State exactly which comparison remains incomplete. Internal consistency can still be assessed. |

OD-S01 Article 25 distinguishes pre-issue recheck from post-print checking and requires return to the original signatory for substantive modifications. OD-S02 Articles 28-29 describe the ministry's recheck and restriction on arbitrary changes after it. Use these provisions at their actual scope; a language assistant does not manufacture a completed approval procedure.

## Correct, recheck and deliver

Use the existing confirmed-error / suggestion / unresolved distinction. Give a material finding's location or exact span, demonstrated conflict/basis, smallest authorized repair or retain decision, and any precise missing decision. Short work needs no compulsory ledger or paperwork.

After a permitted correction, recheck its affected dependencies: actor and responsibility, modal force, condition grouping, negation, exception scope, deadline, shared quantities, protected literals, heading targets and attachment references. Compare in both directions against the governing material. A local repair can require a second location to change; it does not justify unrelated polishing.

Then pass the whole current artifact and available package through the [same shared review loop](review-loop.md). A local recheck is not a full clean review, and this route starts no separate official-document loop. In Audit, the reviewed artifact is the findings/report; an accurate report can retain unresolved defects in its source. Report actual inspected coverage and limits without equating a scanner result, model review or teaching example with practitioner validation or readiness to replace a human proofreader.
