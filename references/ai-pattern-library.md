# Expression, Readers and Author Voice

The historical filename is retained. AI-flavored prose means empty, repetitive or mechanical expression, not an authorship verdict.
Use [meaning preservation](meaning-preservation.md) for common safeguards and post-rewrite comparison.
Apply the shared clarity checks to all prose. Select the edit intents below only when the task includes editing.

## Four clarity levels

| Level | Editorial check |
| --- | --- |
| Sentence meaning | Recover the topic, main predicate, participants, reference, conditions and negation. Inspect unclear noun relations, modifier attachment and comparisons. Repair the located obstacle without changing the claim. |
| Sentence relations | Identify what each substantive addition does: explain, support, exemplify, contrast, qualify, sequence or intentionally change the topic. Supply a warranted missing link. A connective cannot establish an absent premise or cause. |
| Paragraph purpose | Identify the paragraph's contribution to the developing answer. Keep its evidence, explanation and distinct boundaries together. Combine duplicate functions without losing necessary reasoning or orientation. |
| Whole-document structure | Reconstruct the order from the actual content. Check task coverage, prerequisite order, connections between parts and the warranted ending. Repair missing steps and disconnected sections. |

Use reader knowledge and the requested genre to judge clarity. Explain necessary unfamiliar concepts before relying on them.
Do not impose a beginner register, one sentence length or a fixed paragraph pattern on every task.
For Chinese, preserve natural topic progression and recoverable omissions. Restore a noun when competing referents make the intended meaning unclear.
Keep stable terms instead of substituting synonyms for variation. Preserve effective long sentences, necessary repetition and intentional artistic effects.
An editorial check can identify a reading obstacle. It does not establish human comprehension without actual human evidence.

## Select relevant information

For new composition, select relevant detail before expanding it. Do not carry every input sentence into the answer merely because it was supplied.
Keep every fact the task requires and every condition that changes the result. Expression-only editing still preserves all required source meanings.

### Make results and actions easy to find

Give the requested decision or next action in a direct sentence, step or table cell. Do not bury it inside background, repeated warnings or status explanations.
Introduce a batch identifier, person or other unfamiliar label with its role before asking the reader to track it.
A status detail at the end does not repair an identifier that had no context earlier. Add the necessary orientation without inventing what the identifier means.
Group shared criteria once, then compare cases against them. A table can carry values and eligibility without a paragraph repeating every row.
Attach a condition or limit to the claim it changes. Do not restate the same boundary as a separate negative, conclusion and closing summary.
Keep distinct exceptions and decision-changing limits; remove duplicated explanation, not required meaning.
After drafting, locate each requested answer or action in the text. Then test deletion of suspected repeats and check whether any required fact or reasoning is lost.
Reader failure to find an action is a clarity finding even when the words occur somewhere. Literal inclusion alone does not establish useful communication.

## Select the edit intent

An intent identifies the requested outcome. Editing depth identifies how much wording or structure can change.
Use the actual request, reader and source to select the intent. These choices are not additional modes or mandatory intake fields.

| Intent | Appropriate change | Acceptance check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Polish expression | Correct or clarify wording that needs work. Keep effective phrasing. | Grammar and references are clear, with no unsolicited expansion or change of argument. |
| Compress | Combine duplicated meanings and remove empty scaffolding within the requested scope. | The text meets the requested size, with required meanings, distinct limits and inferential connections retained. |
| Expand | Develop supplied observations, definitions or inferential connections for the identified reader. | New substantive assertions have a material basis. Added length advances the explanation without invented cases, data or author decisions. |
| Change register | Explain terms or adjust presentation for the requested reader and genre. | Essential unfamiliar terms have brief, accurate explanations. Quantities, terms, commitment and author judgment remain accurate. |
| Reorganize | Change order or paragraph boundaries to clarify the argument. | Conditions, attribution and quotations remain attached to the claims they govern. Transitions express actual relations. |

Use the shared preservation comparison for every intent. A longer or shorter output alone does not establish success.
Check that actual changes match the requested outcome, not only the heading of a copied instruction card.
For combined goals, apply compatible checks. Do not silently change a compression request into expansion or an edit into research.
If essential constraints conflict, identify the conflict and retain useful faithful work with its actual status.
Do not lose required meanings only to satisfy a length cap. Do not add unsupported material only to reach a target.
Infer routine choices without requiring another user decision. Ask only when an unresolved choice materially affects the result.

## Diagnose function before changing words

Identify the passage's point and the requested editing depth.
For each suspected repeat, qualification, connector or summary, ask what it does for this argument and reader.

| Candidate | Change when | Keep when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broad opening or significance | It adds no needed context or supported judgment. Start from the actual problem. | It establishes a supported contribution or necessary context. |
| Repeated proposition | It repeats meaning and function. Combine or keep the clearer occurrence. | It adds evidence, inference, a distinct boundary or needed section orientation. |
| Qualification | In drafts, it limits no actual claim. In edits, combine duplicated restrictions without losing meaning. | It preserves a distinct original conclusion or changes an actual claim's scope, force or relation. |
| Stock connector | Its stated relation is absent or wrong. Repair order or relation first. | It marks a genuine additive, temporal, contrastive or causal relation. |
| Abstract noun or passive | It obscures a supplied action or referent. | It is a stable technical term or makes the intended focus clearer. |
| Closing summary | The paragraph already completed its job and the ending only restates it. | It supports a further inference or orients an independently readable section. |

Data and the inference from them do different jobs. Do not remove the inferential step as repeated subject matter.
An explanation label or “therefore” does not give a restated conclusion a new function.
For substantial rewriting, try deleting suspected repeats, then check whether evidence, reasoning, scope or orientation was lost.
Check nonadjacent sentences and the opening and ending. Do not replace removed wording with synonyms or invented causes.
Keep each distinct supplied limit in edits. In drafts, attach a limit to the actual claim it affects.
Relevant limitations belong in a requested limitations section. Private checking procedures do not belong in manuscript prose.

### Organize boundaries around the argument

Put a boundary beside the inference it changes. Several adjacent negatives can interrupt the argument even when each is true.
State the supported question and result first, then organize distinct boundaries by their role: measurement, comparison or applicability.
Where the same scope can be expressed in the claim, use it there rather than repeat a defensive closing sentence. Keep every distinct substantive limit in expression edits; reduce the scaffolding, not the meaning.
Do not replace a chain of negatives with a vague umbrella such as “further validation is needed”. It can hide different missing comparisons or measurements.
Write manuscript prose for its scholarly readers. Phrases such as “the supplied material” belong in access or audit notes when they refer to the assistant's inputs; identify the actual studies or evidence in prose.
Such phrases remain valid when supplied material is itself the study's object or their use has a reader function. This is a context decision, not a prohibited-word list.
See [the boundary example](writing-examples.md#12-boundaries-without-an-editorial-ending).

## Reader progression

Check what the intended reader knows, what each sentence adds and what the next sentence takes up.
Repair unclear pronouns, missing inferential links and unexplained changes of topic.
Use context and emphasis to make the author's intended relation recoverable.
Sentence length alone is not a reason to rewrite. Do not add rhythmic variation for its own sake.
Do not replace stable terms with synonyms merely to avoid repetition.

For Chinese prose, check topic continuity and omitted actors across clauses.
A reader must be able to identify who acts, what “这” refers to and which claim a condition restricts.
Repeated technical nouns can clarify a topic change. A long sentence can remain when its relations and scope are clear.
Do not mechanically apply English subject-verb proximity or sentence-final emphasis rules to Chinese syntax.

Synthetic example:

> 线下窗口核对原件，预约页面则提前列出材料要求。申请者可以据此准备材料，但原件是否符合要求仍由窗口判断。

“据此” refers to the listed requirements. “原件” restores the object after the topic changes back to the window.
Replacing both with “这” would blur the information and review roles. No extra closing claim about “important implications” is needed.

### Locate reading obstacles

Judge familiarity for the intended readers and genre. Use the task's register. Everyday conversation is not the required register for academic prose.
A rare combination can need repair when its intended relation is hard to recover. An established technical term can remain unfamiliar to other readers.
Explain a necessary term when the requested readers need help. Keep its construct and use the same term for the same meaning.

| Candidate obstacle | Check and proportionate repair | Preservation control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An unusual word combination | Identify the intended relation. Use a conventional combination with the same meaning. | Do not replace a broad construct with an easier but narrower concept. |
| An odd subject-action combination | Check what action the predicate attributes to the subject in this genre. Repair unfamiliar personification that obscures the supplied action. | Abstract subjects and conventional uses such as “the report recommends” can be valid. Do not invent a person, intention or mental state to restore an actor. |
| A long insertion or nested modifier | Find the main claim and the words its conditions or modifiers govern. Move or unpack material when readers must reconstruct those links. | Keep negation, conditions, attribution and action state attached to the same claim. A comma count does not require division. |
| A dense noun chain | State the relations between its parts, such as object, purpose, source or responsibility. | Resolve only relations supported by the material. Keep an ambiguous relation unresolved or ask when it changes the result. |
| Omitted relation words | Restore a necessary reference, article or connector when the link is hard to follow. | Do not invent a cause, sequence or shared group. In English, an explicit “that” can help mark a clause. |

Reduce empty scaffolding before compressing substantive relations. Extra words can make the same content easier to follow.
One sentence can state a clear main relation with its necessary conditions or comparison. Do not replace this with a one-fact-per-sentence rule.
Consider a compact inline list when readers must apply several conditions together. Keep each condition's force, AND/OR grouping, negation and consequence.
Retain a clear integrated sentence when the list adds no value.
After division or expansion, compare the linked clauses again. Eligibility, an intended action and an action that occurred remain different meanings.

For Chinese, check topic changes, omitted actors, reference and the scope of long modifiers. Do not infer a missing actor merely from an omitted subject.
For English, check distant dependencies, nested relative clauses and noun modifiers whose relation is unclear.
These are contextual checks, not universal sentence limits or a cross-language score.

A text review can locate a phrase that needs rereading. Call it a text review when no audio was used.
If an actual reading aloud or text-to-speech check occurs, record only that action. It does not establish correct understanding.
A model's answers to comprehension questions are proxies. Attribute human understanding only to actual human responses.
For a small edit, keep these checks proportionate and private. Do not append a checklist or comprehension test to prose-only output.
See [the burden examples](writing-examples.md#13-unpack-relations-without-changing-the-construct).

## Author voice from actual samples

Use an available sample as evidence of applicable choices, not as a source of research facts or a fixed imitation template.
For a substantial task, a short profile can record:

| Choice | Evidence and application |
| --- | --- |
| Register and terms | Which wording fits this discipline and reader? Keep stable terms. |
| Stance | How does the author express supported judgments and uncertainty? Preserve their actual commitment. |
| Self-reference and reader guidance | Are first-person claims, questions or explicit signposts part of the useful voice? |
| Sources and argument | How does the author connect citations, observations and interpretation? |
| Information rhythm | How are complex relations unpacked? Use functional patterns, not incidental sentence lengths. |

Record only useful features and their sample basis. Mark uncertain features rather than invent an author persona.
Do not require extra samples for a small edit. Without samples, use the effective draft voice and requested genre.
Do not turn formal prose casual or direct prose ornate without a request.
The same facts can support several good voices. No single short, direct register is the required academic style.
See [the voice alternatives](writing-examples.md#10-same-facts-different-valid-voices).

## Legitimate academic conventions

Generic academic expressions can fulfill real functions. Diagnose their use here rather than their appearance in a phrase list.
Do not copy a source's distinctive formulation or viewpoint to make the prose sound academic.

| Observation | Review question |
| --- | --- |
| 全面赋能 or another strong evaluation | Is the evaluation supported or attributed, and useful here? |
| 大量研究表明 | Which accessible sources support the exact claim and scope? |
| 此外 or another transition | What actual relation does it mark? |
| Parallel clauses or a numbered sequence | Are the facts parallel, or are these genuine process steps? |
| A technical phrase | Is it the established term? Would replacement change its meaning? |
| An exact quotation | Are wording and attribution preserved? Audit its claim separately. |
| A rhetorical question | Does it help this genre and reader or obscure the actual research question? |

Keep clear prose unchanged when it already serves the task.
Do not turn parallel or correlational facts into causes, or hide a baseline to exaggerate novelty.
Perplexity, sentence symmetry, keyword absence and detector scores establish neither writing quality nor AI authorship.

## Scanner findings

The optional scanner marks configured patterns, not all discourse or evidence problems.
Resolve a finding as revise, retain with reason, or needs author decision.
One justified edit and rescan can check whether that match changed. End this scanner subprocess, not the manuscript review loop.
Repeated matches do not justify distorting source meaning. Review attribution and quoted wording separately from heuristic span protection.
