# Chinese Review Examples

All materials below are synthetic teaching examples. They are not extracts from government documents or measured writing results. The disposition depends on the stated context and task mode. More than one faithful repair can be valid.

## 1. Restore a required component from supplied context

Context: 评审对象是《采集说明》。评审组已完成讨论，并提出三条建议。

Draft:

> 评审组完成了对，并提出三条建议。

Expression edit:

> 评审组完成了对《采集说明》的讨论，并提出三条建议。

This repairs the incomplete construction with supplied content. Without the context, do not invent what was discussed. Completion and the three suggestions remain the same.

## 2. Repair the predicate, not the quantity

Context: 记录中的等待时间从四天降至两天。

Draft:

> 记录显示，审批效率提高，等待时间也从四天提高到两天。

Expression edit:

> 记录显示，审批效率提高，等待时间从四天缩短到两天。

The predicate must fit the stated change. Keep both values and the unit; add no cause or population. If the values themselves conflict across sources, report the evidence problem separately.

## 3. Remove an incompatible sentence frame

Draft:

> 系统的优势是能保存历史记录构成的。

Expression edit:

> 系统的优势是能保存历史记录。

The trailing frame has no coherent role. Removing it adds no capability, measurement or completed action. Do not turn the capacity into a claim that a particular record has been saved.

## 4. Clarify modifier attachment when needed

Context: 报告刚刚发布，内容是去年的统计结果。

Draft:

> 请查看刚发布的去年的统计报告。

Optional alternative:

> 请查看刚发布的、关于去年统计结果的报告。

The alternative exposes the intended relation. Do not call every possible ordering of these modifiers a hard grammar error. Keep publication time distinct from the period described.

## 5. Keep a valid topic-comment sentence

> 这份材料，我读过，尚未核查数据。

Retain it when it fits the requested voice. A Chinese topic before the explicit subject is legitimate. “读过” must not become “已核实” or “已批准”. A more formal alternative is optional and must preserve those action states.

## 6. Keep a recoverable omitted actor

Procedure:

> 选中记录后，按“预览”。

The user is the recoverable actor of this instruction. Do not add an organization or reviewer, and do not report that the selection or preview has occurred. An explicit subject in every step can be unnecessary.

## 7. Preserve a necessary condition

> 只有收齐授权书，才可以公开附件。

Do not replace it with “只要收齐授权书，就可以公开附件”. The original makes authorization documents necessary; it does not establish that they are the only requirement. Permission does not establish actual publication.

## 8. Preserve the scope of negation

> 这两份申请不都符合要求。

At least one fails; both may fail. “这两份申请都不符合要求” is a stronger, different assertion. A grammatical rewrite must not determine which application fails or assert that exactly one fails.

## 9. Defer an unknown referent

Source:

> 周敏把李航的申请交给王宁，随后他补交了附件。

Context does not identify the person who added the attachment. In an audit, retain the source and ask which person “他” denotes. In an expression edit, flag the required decision before assigning an actor. Name replacement without evidence is not a grammar repair.

An accurate audit can be complete while that source decision remains open.

## 10. Keep a conventional abstract subject

> 报告指出，现有记录不足以比较两组结果。

Retain this conventional attribution if the report supports it. Do not invent an author interview or a mental state to replace “报告”. Keep the limitation on comparison; it does not establish that either result is false.

## 11. Protect quotation-internal wording

Context: 以下引文须逐字保留；只审查其后的编辑说明。

> 原话为“我对结果没办法不怀疑。”这句话表达了说话者的怀疑。

Retain the exact quotation, including its internal punctuation. Do not automatically remove negatives or replace the quotation with an edited paraphrase. If a task permits a separate paraphrase, label it as a paraphrase and preserve the speaker's force.

## 12. End a statement by its function

Draft:

> 尚不清楚何时恢复预约？

Context: 这是一句陈述，不是向读者提问，也不是带疑问语气的自问。

Expression edit:

> 尚不清楚何时恢复预约。

Apply the speech-act distinction in [S03](chinese-sources.md#inspected-written-form-rules). The word “何时” alone does not make the whole sentence a question. A different context can license a genuine question mark.

## 13. Keep an approximation approximate

Draft:

> 整理工作预计需要三、四周。

Context: 表示三四周左右的概数，不是分别列举第三周和第四周。

Expression edit:

> 整理工作预计需要三四周。

Apply S04 sections 4.2.2 and 5.2.1. Do not choose exactly three or four weeks, add a precise deadline or remove necessary punctuation from an actual enumeration.

## 14. Keep literal data outside numeral normalization

Protected payload:

```json
{"batch":"0019","note":"保留  "}
```

Preserve its leading zeros, keys and the two spaces inside the note. Review the surrounding Chinese explanation separately. A numeral-style preference gives no authority to change this schema or identifier.

## 15. Separate a disputed diagnosis from an optional repair

Context: 编辑已核对两轮；本句只说明这份目录名称写法的统一。

Source:

> 经编辑两轮核对，使这份目录的名称写法统一。

Optional alternative when the editing task calls for a clearer frame:

> 编辑的两轮核对使这份目录的名称写法统一。

The editor and checking event are recoverable. The overlapping opening can motivate a clarity suggestion; the pattern alone does not establish an unrecoverable subject or an uncontested hard error. Do not declare every such opening valid either: inspect the actual predicate and context. Keep the asserted relation, two rounds, this catalogue and the naming outcome. In Audit, retain the source and offer the alternative separately.

## 16. Recover an actor only when the context assigns one

Same instruction: “核对件数后，签收。”

- **Keep:** “以下由收货员办理。货物到达后先查验外包装。核对件数后，签收。” The heading sentence assigns both actions; the imperative needs no repeated subject.
- **Clarify:** “仓管员和采购员均在场。核对件数后，签收。” Presence assigns neither action. Ask who checks and who signs, including whether they are the same person.
- **Correct:** The approved duty sheet assigns counting to the warehouse clerk and signing to the buyer, but the draft reads “仓管员负责收货。核对件数后，签收。” The first sentence cues a single actor for both steps. Write “仓管员核对件数后，由采购员签收。” This repairs the responsibility implied by the draft; it does not diagnose omission in general.

## 17. Check what the negative quantifier denies

Same sentence: “三份申请不都符合条件。”

- **Keep:** Two applications qualify and one does not. “不都” denies that all qualify; changing it to “都不” would make all three fail.
- **Correct the account if authorized:** The confirmed decision sheet says all three fail and the sentence is meant to report that decision. Write “三份申请都不符合条件。” The original is grammatical but fails to convey the established decision. In expression-only work, report this discrepancy separately.
- **Clarify:** The notes say only “至少一份不符合条件”. Keep “不都” if that is the intended claim. If the document must tell staff which applications to return, ask for the individual decisions; the quantifier does not supply them.

## 18. Distinguish an estimate from two specified options

Same sentence: “请预留两三天。”

- **Keep:** A delivery estimate is approximate, with no scheduling choices implied. Retain “两三天” without an enumeration comma under [S04](chinese-sources.md#inspected-written-form-rules).
- **Correct:** The scheduling form requires an explicit choice between two fixed buffers. “请预留两三天” obscures that choice. Write “请在预留两天和预留三天之间选择一项。” The issue is the meaning of the instruction, not a missing comma in the approximation.
- **Clarify:** If the notes do not say whether this is an estimate or a required choice, ask before turning it into a selection or deadline.

## 19. Apply the host mark without normalizing a literal

Same characters: “参数为 `window=2,3`,请重试。”

- **Correct:** In editable Chinese instructions, the comma after the code span joins Chinese clauses. Write “参数为 `window=2,3`，请重试。” The comma in the parameter is part of the literal.
- **Keep:** In a screenshot transcript that must reproduce the same line verbatim, retain both commas. Flag the outer comma separately if reviewing the interface copy; the transcript records what appeared.

## 20. Separate a recommended word form from a quotation

Same wording: “欢迎参预讨论。”

- **Suggest or apply house style:** In an editable notice adopting the table's recommended forms, write “欢迎参与讨论。” The entry under C in [S10](chinese-sources.md#s10-gf-1001-2001-recommended-word-forms) supports that word-form choice. The original syntax does not fail.
- **Keep:** If an archive quotes a notice reading “欢迎参预讨论。” verbatim, retain its word form and punctuation. Apply the house choice only to the surrounding editable prose.

## 21. Distinguish a noun phrase from an instruction

Same span: “仔细的核对”。

- **Keep:** “仔细的核对花了十分钟。” Here “核对” names the activity that took ten minutes; “仔细的” modifies that activity.
- **Correct:** “请仔细的核对编号。” Here “核对” is the instructed action and takes “编号” as its object. Write “请仔细核对编号。” In this sentence “仔细” describes how to perform the action; dropping “的” repairs the relation without changing the instruction.

These examples illustrate decisions. They do not supply the answers to an unrelated evaluation task or prove that every future output will be correct.
