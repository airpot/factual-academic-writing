# Meaning Preservation

Use these shared safeguards in all modes. A light edit can keep the comparison in working context.
For a substantial rewrite, keep a compact record of high-risk meanings. Reuse existing author or claim records.

## New composition and source fidelity

In a new answer, correct the assistant's own errors, including an error carried from earlier conversation.
Do not preserve an incorrect assertion merely for continuity. Explain a material correction when the user needs it to understand the result.
Distinguish established facts, interpretations, assumptions and recommendations. Keep verification appropriate to the task and its source limits.

In expression-only editing, the source remains the meaning target. Preserve its assertions and report proposed evidence corrections separately.
Apply substantive corrections only when the task authorizes them. Do not add an unprovided premise to make the source argument appear complete.
In an audit, assess support and propose corrections without treating that assessment as permission to rewrite the source.

A clearly hypothetical teaching example can introduce invented participants or values to explain a concept.
Requested fiction can introduce imagined events. Keep the fictional frame and supplied creative constraints clear.
Do not present either as observed evidence, a real source or a completed execution. Hypothetical content does not authorize invented nonfiction claims.

## Shared safeguards

- Invent no factual evidence, findings, references, mechanisms, methods or author decisions. Keep hypothetical and fictional content within its stated frame.
- Keep actors, quantities, units, dates, negation, conditions, exceptions, scope and logical relations.
- Preserve direct quotations, including internal punctuation. Do not silently shorten them or move punctuation.
- Keep source identifiers, code, formulas and established terms unless their correction is requested.
- Keep the author's research question and supplied judgment. A proposed reading is not established author intent.
- Keep correlation, causation, sequence and parallel facts distinct.
- Preserve commitment in both directions. Do not make possible actions completed or definite capacities conjectural.
- Keep each distinct supplied substantive limit in expression editing. Combine only duplicate meanings with the same clear scope.

## Identify protected meanings

Record only fields needed for this task. Not every claim needs every field.

| Field | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Actor and action state | Who did, can do, prepared, intends or might do what? |
| Commitment | Is this an observation, capacity, possibility, interpretation or proposal? |
| Condition and scope | Under which condition, population, period or exception does the statement hold? |
| Quantity basis | Which numerator, denominator, unit, overlap, input version and rounding apply? |
| Relation and judgment | What links evidence to the claim, and what position does the author take? |
| Protected wording | Which quotations, definitions, terms, code or formulas must remain exact? |

In expression-only work, the original is the meaning target, even when its evidence is questionable.
Report a supported evidence correction separately. Apply it only when the task includes evidence correction.
In drafting and evidence audits, distinguish source observations, author interpretations and agent proposals with the evidence model.

## Compare in both directions

1. From revision to material: identify the basis of each substantive assertion, including premises, relative clauses and background comparisons.
   A supplied new capability does not establish what the old workflow lacked.
   Missing information in supplied material does not establish that a study omitted the procedure or measurement.
2. From material to revision: locate each required meaning, author judgment and distinct limit in the revised text.
   Check for omissions disguised as concise expression or improved factual precision.
3. Check relations after splitting or reordering. A leading possibility marker does not qualify every later clause automatically.
4. Read the whole revised artifact. Check that restored meanings fit its argument, referents and requested voice.

A source's uncertain explanation can have a definite consequence if that explanation holds.
Keep uncertainty at the explanation. Preserve the stated conditional consequence without adding uncertainty to it.
When the consequence itself is possible, preserve that separate possibility too.
Possible confounders remain possible. A limitation's premise needs the same care as the main result.
An asserted capacity remains a capacity. Preparation, a request, an imagined action and completion remain different states.
Distinguish asserted capacity from hypothetical capacity. Preserve the source's contextual force, such as “can” versus hypothetical “could”.
“The key could fit in the envelope” must not become “the key is small enough to fit” when the fit remains hypothetical.
Check abstract restatements too. Wording about size or readiness can silently strengthen a possibility without using an explicit completion verb.

## Compact comparison example

Synthetic material: a library page lists required identification before collection.
The author states that this page can inform readers before a visit.
Some readers may miss the message. The library has prepared a printed notice, but has not posted it.
No prior notification process is supplied.

| Proposed wording | Decision and basis |
| --- | --- |
| The page moved notification from the desk to the home. | Revise. The old notification location is not supplied. |
| The page might inform readers before a visit. | Revise. This weakens the asserted capacity into conjecture. |
| The library posted a printed notice. | Revise. Preparation is not posting. |
| The page can inform readers before a visit, although some may miss the message. | Retain. Capacity and possible non-use remain distinct. |

Before calling the passage complete, also retain preparation of the notice if that fact serves the requested argument.
Supporting every surviving sentence is insufficient when a required meaning has disappeared.

## Numerical and quotation checks

For calculations, retain inputs, denominator, units, assumptions, source versions and relevant rounding.
A percentage-point difference is not a relative percentage change. Different samples do not establish individual change.
Arithmetic alone establishes neither significance nor causation. Keep justified statistical conclusions and their supplied method and scope.
Exact string comparison can help check quotations or fixed identifiers. Review the context and attribution separately.
Use appropriate mechanical checks when available. They do not establish source truth or research quality.

For prose-only output, express preserved uncertainty in the claim. Do not narrate this checking procedure in the manuscript.
